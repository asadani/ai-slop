# Source Ledger

## LinkedIn Help — “Best practices for content created with the help of AI”

<https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1123063>

Primary source for the “Seems like AI slop” feedback option and LinkedIn's own
definition. It explicitly distinguishes AI-assisted work from low-effort,
generic content without a clear point of view or substance.

## Kommers et al. — “Why Slop Matters” (2025)

<https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06060>

Primary research source for the claim that AI slop has no settled formal
definition. The authors offer a family-resemblance account: superficial
competence, asymmetric effort, and mass producibility. Their argument also
prevents a simplistic definition: some content labeled slop can have personal,
social, or aesthetic value.

## Reuters Institute — “AI-generated slop is quietly conquering the internet”

<https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/news/ai-generated-slop-quietly-conquering-internet-it-threat-journalism-or-problem-will-fix-itself>

Useful reporting on the popular use of the term, its connection to SEO-driven
content farms, and the related idea of “careless speech.” The essay uses it only
for the modest claim that the term is used across media and associated with
cheap, scalable content production.

## NewsGuard — “Tracking AI-enabled Misinformation”

<https://www.newsguardtech.com/special-reports/ai-tracking-center/>

Primary source for the content-farm example. Its tracker describes unreliable
AI-generated news sites operating with little or no human oversight and their
connection to programmatic-advertising incentives.

## AI Slop and the Software Commons (2026)

<https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16754>

An early software-focused paper that frames AI-generated contribution volume as
an externalized cost on reviewers, maintainers, shared knowledge, and trust.

## “An Endless Stream of AI Slop” (2026)

<https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27249>

Qualitative analysis of developer discussions. Used narrowly for its reported
themes of review friction and perceived quality degradation.
